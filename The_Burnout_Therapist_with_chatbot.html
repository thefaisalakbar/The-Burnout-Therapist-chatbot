About Us

We're Lauren and Jenna, two therapists in private practice who met over ten years ago whilst studying, compared notes, and realised we'd been having the same conversations separately ever since.

Jenna — BABCP-Accredited Therapist, Clinical Lead at Priory Hospital, BPS Clinical Supervisor. Specialist in PTSD, trauma, and rebuilding safety. Beyond titles, she deeply values honest human connection.

Lauren — BACP-Accredited Psychotherapist, BPS Clinical Supervisor specialising in burnout, anxiety, and people-pleasing. She runs a group practice in Ayrshire — there's a reason she's the burnout therapist. She gets it.

We built Modern Therapy Collective because we kept wishing it existed. Not to teach you how to be a better therapist — you already are one. We're here because doing this job is better when you don't do it alone.

“move away from perfection and towards authenticity, where we can show up as we are, because meaningful change starts when we feel truly seen and understood.”

— Lauren & Jenna, founders

FAQs

What is Modern Therapy Collective?

MTC is a community for therapists in private practice offering group supervision, CPD, workshops, and ongoing peer support. If you're feeling isolated and looking for connection, you're in the right place.

Does group supervision count towards my hours?

Yes. Our group supervision is led by qualified supervisors and counts towards your CPD and supervision hours.

Where are in-person events?

Launch event in Ayrshire (Scotland). Future in-person gatherings will be based in Scotland. Online membership is available for therapists outside Scotland.

What's the difference between the three tiers?

Reflect: recorded CPD + community. Connect: adds online group supervision. Thrive: includes quarterly in-person gatherings, welcome gift and full supervision.
